# AVNEX Surface

## Intelligent Aerodrome Safeguarding & Surface Generation

**AVNEX Surface** is an advanced aerodrome safeguarding, obstacle evaluation and protection surface generation platform designed to automate the creation, analysis, continuous monitoring and management of aviation protection surfaces.

It brings together **ICAO Annex 14, ICAO Annex 10 (Aeronautical Telecommunications), ICAO Annex 15 (eTOD), ICAO Doc 8168 (PANS-OPS), ICAO Doc 9881 (eTOD Manual), FAA Part 77 and international safeguarding methodologies** within a fully automated, configurable digital environment.

---

### Core Surface Modules & Safeguarding Domains

#### 1. Obstacle Limitation Surfaces (OLS) & Safeguarding Envelopes
* **ICAO Annex 14 Volume 1**: Code 1A to 4F runway classifications (Non-Instrument, Non-Precision, Precision Cat I / II / III).
* **Approach Surfaces**: Horizontal distance, divergence rate, nominal slope (e.g., 2.0% / 1:50 for Code 4), and first/second/horizontal sections.
* **Take-Off Climb Surfaces (TOCS)**: Take-off safety zones, initial divergence (12.5% / 15%), and obstacle assessment planes.
* **Transitional & Inner Transitional Surfaces**: Slope ratios (1:7, 1:5, 1:3) extending to the inner horizontal surface.
* **Inner Horizontal & Conical Surfaces**: Circular and oval radius envelopes (4,000m / 45m height AGL) and ascending conical gradient (5%).
* **Obstacle Free Zone (OFZ) & Obstacle Free Surfaces (OFS)**: Inner approach, inner transitional, and balked landing protection envelopes.
* **FAA 14 CFR Part 77 Subpart C**: Primary surface, approach surface, transitional surface, horizontal surface, and conical surface.

#### 2. Communication, Navigation & Surveillance (CNS) Facilities Surfaces
AVNEX Surface generates high-precision electromagnetic safeguarding envelopes and clearance zones for both **Directional** and **Omnidirectional** CNS facilities in accordance with **ICAO Annex 10, Doc 9157 (Aerodrome Design Manual), and ICAO EUR DOC 015**:

* **Directional CNS Facilities Surfaces**:
  * **Instrument Landing System - Localizer (ILS LOC)**:
    * Critical Areas (no vehicles/structures permitted during approach operations).
    * Sensitive Areas (stringent height & vehicle restrictions to prevent beam multipath & signal bending).
    * Clearance & Evaluation Envelopes: Azimuth coverage sector (±35° up to 10–18 NM, ±10° up to 18–25 NM).
  * **Instrument Landing System - Glide Path (ILS GP)**:
    * Reflection Plane & Forward Ground Zone (smooth ground requirement for image antenna formation).
    * Critical and Sensitive Zones: Lateral and longitudinal wedge extending from mast to threshold.
    * Glide Path Protection Surface (0.75θ to 1.75θ elevation coverage where θ is nominal 3.0° glide angle).
  * **Microwave Landing System (MLS)**: Azimuth and Elevation antenna coverage and reflection protection envelopes.
  * **Precision Approach Radar (PAR)**: Directional sector antenna beam line-of-sight and clearance corridors.
  * **Point-to-Point Directional Radio Links (VHF / UHF / Microwave)**: First and second Fresnel zone ellipsoid clearance calculations between transmitter and receiver towers.

* **Omnidirectional CNS Facilities Surfaces**:
  * **VHF Omnidirectional Range (VOR) / Doppler VOR (DVOR)**:
    * Zone 1 (0 – 300m): Strict cylindrical keep-out zone (flat, clear ground around counterpoise).
    * Zone 2 (300m – 3,000m): Conical evaluation surface with ascending gradient (typically 1.0° to 1.2° for conventional VOR, up to 2.5° for DVOR).
    * Zone 3 (3,000m – 15,000m): Outer multipath scattering evaluation zone for large structures, wind turbines, and power lines.
  * **Distance Measuring Equipment (DME) & Non-Directional Beacon (NDB)**: 360° omnidirectional radial clearance cylinders and overhead cone of silence protection.
  * **Surveillance Radars (PSR, SSR, MSSR, Mode-S)**:
    * Primary Surveillance Radar (PSR) 360° radar line-of-sight (RLOS) coverage cone and false-echo reflection zone.
    * Secondary Surveillance Radar (SSR / MSSR) interrogation line-of-sight and cone-of-cone suppression analysis.
    * Airport Surface Detection Equipment (ASDE / SMR) and Multilateration (MLAT) mast coverage and shadowing analysis.
  * **VHF/UHF Air-Ground Voice Communications (Tx/Rx Stations)**: 360° horizon line-of-sight clearance, terrain masking computation, and electromagnetic interference (EMI) envelopes.

#### 3. electronic Terrain and Obstacle Data (eTOD) — All Areas (1, 2, 3, and 4)
Fully compliant with **ICAO Annex 15 (Aeronautical Information Services), ICAO Doc 9881 (eTOD Manual), and ICAO Doc 10066 (PANS-AIM)**:

* **eTOD Area 1 (Entire State / National Territory)**:
  * Macro-scale digital terrain elevation model (DEM) and obstacle database for the entire territory.
  * Captures all structures and natural terrain exceeding 100m (300ft) Above Ground Level (AGL).
  * Used for en-route navigation, minimum sector altitude (MSA) determination, and terrain awareness systems.
* **eTOD Area 2 (Terminal Control Area & Aerodrome Vicinity)**:
  * Encompasses a 45 km radius centered on the Aerodrome Reference Point (ARP) or TMA boundary.
  * **Area 2a**: Aerodrome surrounding area (45 km radius) for obstacles ≥ 3m AGL above the aerodrome elevation.
  * **Area 2b**: Take-off climb and approach areas extending 10 km from runway threshold, identifying obstacles with 1.2% slope penetration.
  * **Area 2c**: Lateral area beyond runway strip and Area 2b up to the boundary of Area 2a with 1.2% obstacle penetration assessment.
  * **Area 2d**: Designated obstacle areas outside Areas 2a, 2b, and 2c containing obstacles of operational significance.
* **eTOD Area 3 (Aerodrome Movement Area & Infrastructure)**:
  * High-precision, high-resolution dataset covering the aerodrome movement area, runway strips, taxiways, aprons, de-icing pads, and Obstacle Free Zones (OFZ).
  * Captures terrain and obstacles of height ≥ 0.5m (including taxiway signs, lighting fixtures, blast fences, service vehicles, and mobile stands).
  * Millimeter-level vertical and horizontal precision conforming to AMDB (DO-272/ED-99) standards.
* **eTOD Area 4 (Category II / III Precision Approach Touchdown Zone)**:
  * Ultra-high-density terrain and obstacle profile extending 900m prior to runway threshold across a lateral width of 60m (120m total width).
  * Dedicated to precision radio altimeter operating area (RAOA) terrain profiling for auto-land and flare maneuver certification.
